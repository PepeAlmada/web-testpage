Checkout help

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and PayPal. Apple Pay and Google Pay are supported on compatible devices.

What's your refund policy?

Every paid course comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee. If it's not for you, email us within 30 days of purchase for a full refund — no questions asked.

Will I get a receipt?

Yes — a VAT receipt is emailed automatically the moment your payment clears, and every receipt is available under My account → Billing.

Do you offer discounts?

We run seasonal sales and offer a student discount of 30% with a valid student email. Team and bulk pricing is available on request via the contact page.
