About us

We help creative people
build technical careers

Webtest is an online school for the creative web. Since 2016 we've taught 60,000+ people around the world to design and build their own ideas. Our studio cat is called Pixel, and the team mascot is a robot named Wilson.

Our story

Built by designers, for designers who want to code.

Webtest started as a small Slack group of people teaching each other to code. Today it's a global community of creative makers learning everything from HTML and CSS to React, JavaScript, UX and beyond.

We believe technical skills shouldn't be locked behind dry textbooks or expensive bootcamps. Our courses are practical, friendly and made by people who actually use this stuff to do creative work every day.
